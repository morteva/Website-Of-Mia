A Quiet Hello

If you want to say something, say it. Just read the note first so neither of us has to pretend the internet comes with mind-reading.

A note before you sendClose the note

I read the messages that actually make it through, but I may not reply. That isn’t a game and it does not mean I hated what you wrote. If you want a reply, leave a real email address. If you don’t hear back, don’t invent a hidden message inside the silence. I probably just didn’t answer.

I’m private. This website is me opening a window into my life; it is not me handing strangers the front-door key.

Cruel, threatening, or deliberately fucked-up messages get deleted, blocked, or reported if they need to be. I’m not keeping a museum of abuse.

Messages include the originating IP address for abuse prevention and site security. I don’t sell, rent, trade, or use your information for marketing.

Questions are fine. Suggestions are fine. Tell me what made sense, what didn’t, what helped, or what you think deserves more detail.

You can have an opinion about me. You do not get editing rights over who I am.
