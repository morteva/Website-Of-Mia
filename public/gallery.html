Cars & FabricationMy Short Racing Career
📜Info

I got into cars because I needed somewhere to disappear during an abusive relationship. My first attempt was basically a ricer and people roasted the shit out of it. Fair. So I learned. For about three years I taught myself whatever I could get my hands on: engine rebuilds, custom nitrous systems, wiring harnesses, welding, turbo and supercharger builds, roll cages, fabrication, and electronics. I raced at tracks and I raced illegally. The illegal part eventually got me nine months, and I served it. I broke things, blew engines, built things, fixed things, and had a fucking blast. Cars were exactly what I needed during that chapter of my life. I’ve moved on to things that matter more to me now, but some part of me will always be a car girl.
