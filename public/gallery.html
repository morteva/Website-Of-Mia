Cars & FabricationMy Short Racing Career
📜Info

I started working on cars as an escape from an abusive relationship and fell completely into it. I started with a ricer, got roasted for it, and decided that wasn’t the road I wanted to stay on. So I learned. For the next three years I taught myself everything I could: engine rebuilds, custom nitrous systems, wiring harnesses from scratch, welding, turbo and supercharger builds, roll cages, and pretty much everything in between. I raced at tracks and I raced illegally. I served nine months for the illegal racing. I did the time, and I’m not hiding it. I broke things, I blew engines, I learned how to fix them, and I had a fucking blast doing it. Cars were exactly what I needed during that chapter of my life. Now I’m moving toward bigger things, including This Is Beside™ and work with a much more global reach. But some part of me will always be a car girl.
