🩸 Why I kept building homes

I didn’t become guarded because I never cared about people. I cared too fucking much.

I was stabbed in the stomach by my father when I was 8, and that is not even close to the full story of what I survived growing up. My teenage years and later relationships added plenty more. Somehow, through all of it, I still kept trying to be a good person and help people.

When real life became unbearable, I escaped into virtual worlds. Then I did what apparently comes naturally to me: I started building communities inside them. Guilds became places where I tried to gather good, solid people and create the kind of loyalty and belonging I kept looking for in real life.

Some of those people became family. Others reminded me exactly why I had become so guarded in the first place. After enough betrayal, manipulation, and people taking more than they gave, I eventually reached a point where I was just fucking done with humanity.

“No matter how much shit I went through, I still tried to be a good person and help others.”

And eventually: “Yeah, I was fucking done with humanity until your ass came along.”
