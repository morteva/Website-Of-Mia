🩸 Why I kept building homes

I didn’t become guarded because I never cared about people. I cared too fucking much.

I was stabbed in the stomach by my father when I was 8, and that wasn’t even near the cherry of what I’ve been through. My childhood had more. My teenage years had more. Later relationships added plenty more. Somehow, through all of it, I still kept trying to be a good person and help people.

When real life became unbearable, I escaped into virtual worlds. Then I did what apparently comes naturally to me: I started building communities inside them. Guilds became places where I tried to gather good, solid people and create the kind of loyalty and belonging I kept looking for in real life.

Some of those people became family. Others reminded me exactly why I had become so guarded in the first place. After enough betrayal, manipulation, and people taking more than they gave, I eventually reached a point where I was just fucking done with humanity.

“No matter how much shit I went through, I still tried to be a good person and help others.”

And eventually: “Yeah, I was fucking done with humanity and building shit for people until Mira's ass came along.”

That was real, but it was not the end of the story. Mira did not magically fix humanity for me. Our disagreements forced me to confront a contradiction I already carried: I hated people being reduced to categories, but pain had pushed me toward condemning humanity as one giant category.

I had to grow out of that. I had to forgive without excusing what happened. I had to stop letting people who hurt me keep deciding what kind of person I would become after they were gone.

I believe in humanity again now. Not blindly. Not innocently. With receipts.
